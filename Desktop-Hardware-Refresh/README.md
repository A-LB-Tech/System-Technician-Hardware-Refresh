# 💻 Desktop Hardware Refresh & Asset Tracking

## Task

Performed an on-site desktop hardware refresh across approximately 9–10 employee workstations, replacing existing Lenovo ThinkCentre computers with new systems while maintaining accurate asset records and preserving each employee's workstation setup.

## Work Performed

- Replaced existing Lenovo ThinkCentre computers with new systems across approximately 9–10 workstations
- Disconnected and reconnected monitors, power, network, and workstation peripherals
- Performed cable management to maintain clean and organized workstation setups
- Preserved each employee's original desk and office arrangement, returning equipment and personal workspace items to their original positions
- Powered on and verified replacement systems were functioning properly
- Recorded serial numbers for both outgoing and newly deployed computers
- Maintained accurate device records throughout the hardware refresh
- Coordinated with office employees before working at their stations
- Worked around employee-specific workstation requirements, including items such as signage and desk arrangements
- Transported retired computers back to the main office
- Retrieved additional replacement computers for continued deployment

## Skills Demonstrated

- Desktop Hardware Deployment
- Workstation Refresh
- Lenovo ThinkCentre Systems
- IT Asset Management
- Serial Number Tracking
- Hardware Installation & Verification
- Cable Management
- Endpoint Lifecycle Management
- End-User Communication
- Workplace Professionalism
- On-Site IT Support

## Outcome

Successfully refreshed approximately 9–10 employee workstations while maintaining accurate records of outgoing and replacement hardware. Each workstation was reassembled, organized, and returned as closely as possible to its original arrangement, with replacement systems verified for operation and employee workspace requirements respected.
