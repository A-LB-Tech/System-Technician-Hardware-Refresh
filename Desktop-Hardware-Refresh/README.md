# 💻 Desktop Hardware Refresh & Asset Tracking

## Task

Performed an on-site desktop hardware refresh across multiple client locations, replacing existing Lenovo ThinkCentre computers with new systems while maintaining accurate asset records, supporting employees, and preserving each employee's workstation setup.

## Work Performed

- Replaced **several Lenovo ThinkCentre computers** across multiple employee workstations
- Disconnected and reconnected monitors, power, network connections, and workstation peripherals
- Performed cable management to maintain clean and organized workstation setups
- Preserved each employee's original desk and office arrangement, returning equipment and workspace items to their original positions
- Powered on and verified replacement systems were functioning properly
- Recorded serial numbers for both outgoing and newly deployed computers
- Maintained accurate device records throughout the hardware refresh
- Coordinated with office employees before and during work at their stations
- Accommodated employee-specific workstation requirements and desk arrangements
- Transported retired computers back to the main office
- Retrieved additional replacement computers for continued deployment across client locations

## 🔧 On-Site Employee Support

Alongside the scheduled hardware refresh, assisted employees with technical issues and requests encountered while working on-site.

### Issues Supported

- Troubleshot and assisted with a **non-functioning workstation monitors**
- Troubleshot an **Epson printer that was not printing**
- Communicated directly with employees to understand reported issues
- Investigated workstation and peripheral problems to identify the cause
- Verified equipment functionality following troubleshooting

## Outcome

Successfully refreshed **14 employee workstations** across multiple client locations while maintaining accurate records of outgoing and replacement hardware. Replacement systems were installed, connected, organized, and verified for operation.

Employee workspaces were restored to their original arrangements following deployment, minimizing disruption to users. In addition to the planned hardware refresh, provided on-site technical support for employee workstation and peripheral issues.
